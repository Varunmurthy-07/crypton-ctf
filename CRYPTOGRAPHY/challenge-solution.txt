# Simple Explanation of How We Solved the Cryptography Challenge

## What We Started With
We had 3 big numbers in a file called `message(1).txt`:
1. `n`: A really huge number (kind of like a big lock)
2. `e`: A small number: 20 (this is like a special number used to lock the message)
3. `c`: Another huge number (this is our scrambled/secret message)

## Our Goal
Find the original hidden message!

---

## Step 1: Look for Clues
We noticed `e` was a really small number (20). That made us think: "Maybe the secret message, when multiplied by itself 20 times, is just equal to `c` without any wrapping around the big lock `n`!"

---

## Step 2: Check Our Guess
We needed to find a number `m` (the secret message) such that when you multiply `m` by itself 20 times, you get exactly `c`.

To do this, we used a fast method called **binary search** to find `m` quickly (like guessing a number between 1 and a trillion but only asking 40 questions instead of a trillion!).

---

## Step 3: Turn the Number Back Into Words
Once we found the number `m`, we just needed to convert it back into text!

---

## The Secret Message!
And the hidden message (flag) was: `picoCTF{t1ny_e_63ab67e3}`
